ABOUT BOARDINGFINDER

Find a Place You Can Call Home

BoardingFinder is a platform designed to help students find suitable boarding houses easily and conveniently.

What is BoardingFinder?

BoardingFinder helps students search for boarding houses and find information about available accommodations.

The platform also helps boarding house owners manage their properties and connect with potential tenants.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make the process of finding and managing boarding houses simpler, faster, and more organized.

BoardingFinder provides a convenient place where students and boarding house owners can interact through one platform.
